About

A video editor with one foot in code.

I'm Antonio, a freelance video editor. I help creators, founders and consultants get consistent videos, without having to learn how to edit.

I've been editing videos as a freelancer since 2020, for creators, brands and professionals; for documentaries, short films and company videos I also handle filming and directing. From 2022 to 2024 I handled marketing at FeedEat, a startup born at school.

Everyone has their own way of expressing themselves. Mine is through video.

I hold a degree in Computer Science for Digital Communication from the University of Camerino: that's why, alongside editing, I also write code.

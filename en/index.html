About

A video editor with one foot in code.

I've been editing videos as a freelancer since 2020, for creators, brands and professionals; for documentaries, short films and company videos I also handle filming and directing. From 2022 to 2024 I was Chief Marketing Officer at FeedEat, where I coordinated the creative team.

Everyone has their own way of expressing themselves. Mine is through video.

I hold a degree in Computer Science for Digital Communication from the University of Camerino: that's why, alongside editing, I also write code.

  • Benevento, Italy
  • Freelance since 2020
  • Computer Science for Digital Communication
How I work

Every cut must have room to breathe. I don't just edit videos, I build emotional paths. My goal is to capture the viewer from the first frame and not let go until the last.
